## 6. Money — the true part

YouTube does not pay on day one.

Usually you need one of these:

- **1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours** in 12 months, or
- **10 million Shorts views** in 90 days.

There is a smaller **500-subscriber** level in some countries for memberships and
Supers, but not everywhere.

So ₹100–₹1,000 is a **later milestone**, not a launch-night payment.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must set up **AdSense**.

Do not buy subscribers or views. That can end the channel.
